Qunsheng Zhang 张群声

M.S. in Mathematics · AI Safety Researcher

I received my M.S. degree from the School of Mathematics, Southeast University. My research focuses on trustworthy artificial intelligence, including agent security and large language model safety, multi-agent systems, reinforcement learning, and distributed control.

I worked on security-operation agents at the Head Office of Bank of Communications, where I studied behavior aggregation, hybrid retrieval-augmented generation, multi-agent reasoning, and auditable decision making. Prior to my master's study, I obtained a B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from University of Shanghai for Science and Technology.

I am looking for Ph.D. opportunities and collaborators interested in Trustworthy AI, Agent Security, LLM Safety, and Multi-Agent Systems. I am particularly interested in building intelligent agents that can reason with verifiable evidence, respect execution boundaries, and remain reliable in high-stakes environments. If you are interested in collaboration, please feel free to reach out via email.

Optimized predefined-time control for high-order nonlinear MASs via ICA and reinforcement learning

Qunsheng Zhang, Jianqiang Hu, Jinde Cao, Tianhu Yu

Published in ISA Transactions, 2026 JCR Q1

We develop an identifier–critic–actor reinforcement learning framework for optimized predefined-time control of high-order nonlinear multi-agent systems. Prescribed performance, sliding-mode control, and projection correction are integrated to guarantee practical predefined-time consensus under external disturbances.

Fixed-time synchronization of nonlinear coupling MNNs with time delay via aperiodically intermittent sliding mode control

Qunsheng Zhang, Jianqiang Hu, Jinde Cao

Published in Communications in Nonlinear Science and Numerical Simulation, 2025 JCR Q1

We study fixed-time synchronization for nonlinear coupled memristive neural networks with time delay. An aperiodically intermittent sliding-mode controller is designed, and sufficient synchronization conditions together with an explicit settling-time bound are established.
