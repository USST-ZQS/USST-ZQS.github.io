Optimized predefined-time control for high-order nonlinear MASs via ICA and reinforcement learning

Qunsheng Zhang, Jianqiang Hu, Jinde Cao, Tianhu Yu

Published in ISA Transactions, 2026 JCR Q1

This work presents an identifier–critic–actor reinforcement learning framework for optimized predefined-time control of high-order nonlinear multi-agent systems. Prescribed performance, sliding-mode control, and projection correction are combined to achieve practical predefined-time consensus under disturbances while minimizing a long-term performance index.

Fixed-time synchronization of nonlinear coupling MNNs with time delay via aperiodically intermittent sliding mode control

Qunsheng Zhang, Jianqiang Hu, Jinde Cao

Published in Communications in Nonlinear Science and Numerical Simulation, 150, 108995, 2025 JCR Q1

This paper studies fixed-time synchronization of nonlinear coupled memristive neural networks with time delay. An aperiodically intermittent sliding-mode control scheme is designed, and sufficient synchronization conditions with an explicit settling-time estimate are derived.

VERA: Root-Domain Evidence Quorums for Carrier Integrity in Tool-Calling Agents

Qunsheng Zhang

Under Review at AAAI 2027 Under Review

VERA introduces root-domain evidence quorums and an execution-boundary monitor for tool-calling agents. The mechanism verifies signed evidence, semantic claims, task scope, provenance dependency closure, and exact invocation capabilities, supported by controlled experiments and finite-root model checking.
